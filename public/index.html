        Explore a sample relief operation, from incident reporting to resource allocation and delivery. These actions update the demo workspace.

        Live Feature: AI Voice Radio Distress Dispatch Librosa AI
        Simulates receiving a high-distress Mayday radio call. Librosa extracts MFCC, Pitch, Energy & Speech Rate; automatically flags critical panic override & triggers re-allocation!
        Step 1: Baseline Reset
        Resets to 4 mixed-tier zones. Ward 3 holds scarce 12 medical kits.
        Step 2: Submit Critical Ward 9 Report
        New zone with trapped residents triggers hard rescue override → critical. Reallocation agent proposes diverting 12 medical kits from Ward 3.
        Step 3: Accept Re-allocation Proposal
        Coordinator accepts diversion. Stock re-targets to Ward 9 without stock loss. Inventory decrements upon delivery confirmation.
        Step 4: Surge Ward 7 Population (x2)
        Ward 7 population doubles (800 → 1600). Severity score surges live; reallocation check evaluates and logs provable no-op.
        Step 5: Resubmit Ward 3 (Duplicate Report Detection)
        Duplicate report detector flags match score ≥ 3. Dashboard displays merge/dismiss review banner.
        Step 6: Depleted Stock Claim (Honest Shortfall)
        Ward 14 claims medical supplies with empty inventory. System issues partial allocation and honestly audits the unmet gap.
        Step 7: Audit Trace & SITREP Inspection
        Navigates to SITREP & Audit Log verifying complete traceability across all responding agencies.
